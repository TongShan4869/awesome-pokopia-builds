## Build notes

This full-build frame is the kind of hero screenshot the gallery should preserve: the whole composition is visible at once, with stacked pastel homes, roof gardens, stairs, lamps, balconies, and a canal cutting through the foreground.

The strongest design move is the vertical town layout. Each roof becomes another terrace, so the build reads like a compact seaside neighborhood instead of a single house. The water channel and stone wall give the scene a clean base, while the banners, awnings, plants, and warm lighting make it feel lived in.
