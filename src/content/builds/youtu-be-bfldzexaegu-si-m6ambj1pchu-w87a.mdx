## Build Notes

HorribleGaming's coastal town habitat turns a small raised block into a compact seaside shopping street. Two connected facades mix pale siding, brickwork, storefront windows, layered roofs, awnings, flower boxes, and climbing vines, while the balcony and side stairs give the build a convincing second level.

The lower plaza is what makes the composition feel like a town rather than a single house. Broad stone steps, paired streetlights, waterfront-style rope posts, a small seating nook, and the angled park edge create a clean public entrance without crowding the shopfronts.
