## Build Notes

Zoibean's Honey Café habitat wraps a warm cottage-style facade around a relaxed outdoor tea garden. The front combines mosaic tiling with timber walls, layered roof pieces, striped awnings, and two rounded rooftop rooms, while the yard adds barrels, flower beds, hanging lights, lounge seating, and low tables set for tea.

The creator's recommended-items slides include the structural pieces for recreating the cafe shell and roofline, plus grass flooring, window panes, wooden enclosures, and crates for the garden details.
