## Build Notes

This seaside market street uses a long row of pastel shopfronts to create a bright harbor promenade. The build layers colorful tiled roofs, striped awnings, vines, flower boxes, produce displays, stone paving, and waterfront railings so the shops feel busy without losing the clean coastal read.

The opening showcase frames it as a complete harbor district, with Pokemon gathered along the market walk and the waterline pulling the whole scene together.
